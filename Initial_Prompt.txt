Create the following models in dashboard/models.py:

1. Activity
   - name: CharField
   - category: CharField (choices: MUAY_THAI, GYM, DOG_TRAINING, KITESURFING, AI_DEV_CORE, AI_DEV_BUSINESS, AI_DEV_MACOS, AI_DEV_TOOLS, CUSTOM)
   - scheduled_date: DateField
   - start_time: TimeField (optional for flexible AI blocks)
   - end_time: TimeField (optional for flexible AI blocks)
   - duration_minutes: IntegerField (auto-calculated or manually logged)
   - status: CharField (choices: PLANNED, IN_PROGRESS, DONE, SKIPPED, RESCHEDULED)
   - notes: TextField (optional)
   - created_at: DateTimeField(auto_now_add=True)

2. AIProject
   - name: CharField (e.g., PersonalLifeOS, fulofilo-analytics, CORTEX)
   - category: CharField (choices: CORE_OS, BUSINESS_DATA, MACOS_NATIVE, TOOLS_SCRIPTS)
   - priority: CharField (choices: HIGH, MEDIUM, LOW)
   - status: CharField (choices: ACTIVE, PAUSED, COMPLETED)
   - github_repo: URLField (optional)

3. ProjectSession (For time tracking AI projects)
   - project: ForeignKey(AIProject)
   - activity: ForeignKey(Activity, null=True, blank=True)
   - start_time: DateTimeField
   - end_time: DateTimeField (null=True, blank=True)
   - duration_minutes: IntegerField (auto-calculated on end)
   - notes: TextField (optional)

4. DailyPlan
   - date: DateField (unique)
   - morning_intention: TextField
   - evening_review: TextField
   - mood_score: IntegerField (1-10, optional)
   - energy_score: IntegerField (1-10, optional)
   - created_at: DateTimeField(auto_now_add=True)

5. Habit
   - name: CharField
   - description: TextField (optional)
   - frequency: CharField (choices: DAILY, WEEKLY, CUSTOM)
   - target_days: JSONField (e.g. ["MON","WED"] for Muay Thai)
   - streak_count: IntegerField (default=0)
   - is_active: BooleanField (default=True)

6. KiteSession
   - date: DateField
   - location: CharField
   - wind_speed_knots: FloatField (optional)
   - duration_minutes: IntegerField
   - notes: TextField (optional)

7. TelegramReminder
   - activity: ForeignKey(Activity, null=True, blank=True)
   - message: TextField
   - remind_at: DateTimeField
   - is_sent: BooleanField (default=False)
   - chat_id: CharField
